      Branding

      Team name, logo and accent color show everywhere — page headers, the browser tab, buttons and active tabs — for every visitor. Logos: PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, up to 1 MB.

      Scoring Settings

      These control every player's Total Points and the team's League Points, everywhere in the app — past weeks are recalculated too, not just new ones.

      BYE weeks keep working the same regardless of these settings: they still earn "points per frame won" for the score recorded on that week, but never the match-win bonus.

      Other
